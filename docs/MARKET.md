# Brief

Who: Shopify sellers running Meta ads to cold traffic, from dropshippers to small DTC brands.
Pain: clicks but no sales because the page opens generic while the ad sold one idea; they duplicate product pages or build Replo/GemPages pages per angle and lose track of what converts.
Market (Nov 2026): UTM Customizer $25, Relevant Bits $99-399, Shogun Personalization $199, InfluenceOwl, Auware $199, Intelligems $69-349 (A/B, not ad match), AdSwitch $19-149 (closest). All key off UTMs or hand-built links; none use Meta ad IDs, auto-discover ads, or report revenue per ad with a holdout.
Wants from reviews and Reddit: true no-code, no code left in theme, fast pages, clean read on what converts, responsive support.
